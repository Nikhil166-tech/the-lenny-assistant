# Episode: Brian Chesky on Founder Mode, Product Craft, and the 11-Star Experience
**Guest:** Brian Chesky (Co-founder and CEO of Airbnb)
**Host:** Lenny Rachitsky
**Date:** 2024-09-02
**Topic:** Founder Mode, Eliminating Bureaucracy, End-to-End Product Design, 11-Star Experience

---

### Segment 1: The Trap of Professional Management and Founder Mode [00:05:30]
Lenny: Brian, your recent talk at YC ignited the entire tech industry about 'Founder Mode'. What did you learn that changed how you run Airbnb?

Brian Chesky: In 2019, right before COVID, Airbnb had grown huge. People kept telling me: "Brian, you are a scaled company now. You need to hire professional executives, empower them, delegate everything, and get out of the details." So I did. And it was a disaster. The product fragmented. We had 10 different business units doing things that didn't talk to each other. When COVID hit and our business dropped 80% in eight weeks, I took back control.
Founder mode means you are in the details. You do not abdicate responsibility to middle management. You run the company as a unified product, not a federation of fiefdoms. Steve Jobs was famous for this—he reviewed every single product release every Wednesday.

### Segment 2: The 11-Star Experience Framework [00:22:15]
Lenny: How do you design products that people genuinely fall in love with?

Brian Chesky: We have an exercise we call the 11-Star Experience. If you want to build something extraordinary, don't ask: "How do we get 5 stars?"
Instead, push the idea to the absurd extreme:
- A 5-star experience: You check into your Airbnb, the key is where it said it would be, the room is clean.
- A 6-star experience: There is a handwritten note and a bottle of wine.
- A 7-star experience: The host knows you love surfing and left a surfboard and a pass to the best beach.
- A 10-star experience: You arrive at the airport, there is a Beatles-style motorcade, 5,000 cheering fans, and the President greets you.
Obviously you cannot do a 10-star experience at scale. But when you explore the absurd 8-star or 9-star ideas, you realize the 6-star and 7-star parts are actually easy and inexpensive to implement. That is where iconic products are born.

### Segment 3: Eliminating Product Managers Who Act Like Bureaucrats [00:38:00]
Lenny: You caused a stir when you said Airbnb eliminated traditional product management. What did you replace it with?

Brian Chesky: We didn't eliminate the function; we combined Product Management with Product Marketing. We stopped hiring PMs who were just human status routers. A PM who just writes tickets, attends standups, and doesn't know design or user empathy is dead weight. We elevated product designers to equal partners with engineering and insisted that leaders be craftspeople who love the product.
