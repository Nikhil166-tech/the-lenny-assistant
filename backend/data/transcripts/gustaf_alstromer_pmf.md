# Episode: Gustaf Alströmer on How to Know If You Have Product-Market Fit
**Guest:** Gustaf Alströmer (Group Partner at Y Combinator, former Head of Growth at Airbnb)
**Host:** Lenny Rachitsky
**Date:** 2023-03-22
**Topic:** Product-Market Fit (PMF), Y Combinator Criteria, Retention Benchmarks

---

### Segment 1: The Definition of Real Product-Market Fit [00:05:10]
Lenny: Gustaf, you have seen thousands of YC startups. How do you define product-market fit?

Gustaf Alströmer: PMF is when users are pulling the product out of your hands. Before PMF, you are pushing a boulder uphill—every user requires immense persuasion, marketing, and hand-holding. After PMF, you feel like you are chasing the boulder down the hill. Servers are crashing, customers are demanding features, and people get genuinely angry when your service goes down.
At YC, we look at one primary chart: Cohort Retention. If month after month, 20% to 40% of the cohort continues to use the product and pay for it, you have PMF. If the line drops to zero, no amount of growth hacking or advertising will save you.

---

### Segment 2: Retention Benchmarks by Category [00:16:40]
Lenny: What are the target retention numbers founders should aim for in different industries?

Gustaf Alströmer: Here are the approximate benchmarks we look for at YC:
1. Consumer Social / Apps: 25% to 35% Day-30 retention.
2. B2B SaaS (SMB): 60% to 70% Annual net revenue retention.
3. B2B Enterprise SaaS: 100%+ Net Dollar Retention (NDR) due to seat and tier expansion.
4. Marketplace / E-commerce: 20% to 30% monthly repeat transaction rate.

If you are below these numbers, stop hiring sales reps and go back to talking to your users every single day.
